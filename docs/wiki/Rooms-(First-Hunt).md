To view or export a room, use the value of the Name column.

Rows with the same Archive value load the same model, animation, and collision files, but may have different entity and nodedata files.

3 | e3Level | Level SP Morphball | Training | Morph Ball
4 | blueRoom | Level SP Regulator | Training | Regulator
5 | mp2 | Level SP Survivor | Training | Survivor
6 | testLevel | Level FhTestLevel | Unused | Test level
7 | mp5 | Level MP5 | Unused | Early Head Shot
9 | e3Level | E3 level | Unused | Stasis Bunker
